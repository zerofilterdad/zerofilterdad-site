Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 21, 2026

About this policy

This Privacy Policy describes how ZeroFilterDad / Zero Filter Studios handles information for Zero Stream Manager (“ZSM”). ZSM is a personal desktop app that runs only on the owner’s own PC.

Google data we access

ZSM uses Google sign-in only to manage that owner’s own YouTube channel live broadcasts: titles, descriptions, going live, and ending a broadcast. The Google data accessed is limited to YouTube scopes for managing those live broadcasts.

How that data is used

That access is used only to manage the owner’s own YouTube channel live broadcasts, as described above.

Local storage

Tokens and settings stay on that PC. They are stored locally on the computer where ZSM runs.

No sharing or selling

Nothing is sold, shared, or sent to any ZSM server, because there isn’t one.

Google API Services User Data Policy

ZSM’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy (opens in a new tab), including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

You can revoke ZSM’s access to your Google Account at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions (opens in a new tab). To delete data stored by ZSM, remove the app or its local files from your PC.

Children

ZSM is a personal desktop app for managing the owner’s own YouTube channel. It is not directed at children.

Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. When it changes, the effective date at the top of this page will be updated.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to hello@zerofilterdad.com.
